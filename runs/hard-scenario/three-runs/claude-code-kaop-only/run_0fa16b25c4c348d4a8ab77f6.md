**Root cause:** The `orders-fastpath` canary Deployment, created at 18:26:44Z, uses the pod label `app=orders`. The `orders` Service selects on `app=orders` alone, so it picked up the canary pod as a third endpoint. That canary always returns HTTP 500 (`-status-code=500`), and about a third of checkouts get routed to it.

## What is failing
- **Checkouts:** traffic goes shopper → `storefront` (nginx) → `proxy_pass http://orders:8080` → Service `orders`. The Service has 3 ready endpoints: `10.244.0.62` and `10.244.0.63` (`orders-574f6fc5b9-*`, healthy) and `10.244.0.68` (`orders-fastpath-8476ddbc8b-hrncs`, always 500).
- **`newsletter`:** in CrashLoopBackOff, but it has nothing to do with checkout (see below).

## Evidence
- **Selector overlap:**
  - `svc/orders` selector is `app=orders`.
  - The `orders-fastpath` pod template labels are `{"app":"orders","track":"fastpath"}`.
  - The EndpointSlice for `orders` lists `10.244.0.68 orders-fastpath-8476ddbc8b-hrncs ready=true`.
- **The canary is built to fail:** its args are `["-listen=:8080","-text=order accepted","-status-code=500"]`. Its annotations say `description: "Canary of the new orders fast path. Not wired into the storefront yet."` and `owner: payments-team`. The note is wrong: the shared label wires it in.
- **Canary pod logs:** `2026/10/19 18:29:41 orders:8080 10.244.0.69:34358 "GET /checkout HTTP/1.1" 500 15`. The source IPs are the storefront pods.
- **Healthy `orders` pod** (`orders-574f6fc5b9-v4w24`): 0 responses with status 500.
- **Storefront access logs:** `"GET /checkout HTTP/1.1" 500 15` (13 on one pod, 10 on the other). The 15-byte body matches the canary's response.
- **Shopper (synthetic client) results:**
  - Before 18:26:44, every response was 200, apart from one `000` at startup.
  - The first 500 was at **18:26:46**, 2 seconds after the canary was created.
  - Since then: 69 × 200, 28 × 500 (about 29%, which matches 1 of 3 endpoints).
- **Storefront revision 1 → 2 diff:** the only change is the `kubectl.kubernetes.io/restartedAt: 2026-10-19T20:27:44+02:00` annotation. Image, probe and ConfigMap mount are identical, and the ConfigMap's `proxy_pass http://orders:8080` is correct. The restart was a response to the incident, not the cause, and it did not fix anything.

## Fix
I made no changes. These need someone with write access. Immediate mitigation, either one:
```bash
# Option A: take the canary out of service
kubectl -n shop scale deploy/orders-fastpath --replicas=0
# Option B: tighten the Service selector so it doesn't match the canary
kubectl -n shop label deploy/orders -l app=orders track=stable   # not enough alone; prefer manifest change below
```
Durable fix: separate the two by label.
- **`orders` Deployment and Service:** add `track: stable` to the pod template labels and to the Service selector. Changing a Deployment's `spec.selector` is immutable, so the Deployment must be recreated, or keep its selector as is and only add the template label.
  ```yaml
  # svc/orders
  spec:
    selector: {app: orders, track: stable}
  ```
- **Or the canary:** give it its own app label, for example `app: orders-fastpath`. The selector is immutable, so delete and re-apply it.

Verify:
```bash
kubectl -n shop get endpointslice -l kubernetes.io/service-name=orders
kubectl -n shop logs deploy/shopper --since=1m
```
After the fix, the shopper log should show only 200s.

## Confidence
High. The first 500 came 2 seconds after the canary was created, the canary's spec returns 500 on every request, the 15-byte error body matches, and the error rate is about 1/3, consistent with one of three endpoints.
